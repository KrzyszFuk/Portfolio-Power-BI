Sales Report– Power BI Data Analysis Project
Type: Educational / Portfolio Project

Business Problem:
The company faced a significant revenue decline in 2023 after peak performance in 2022. Leadership needed a comprehensive, multi-dimensional view of sales trends, margin health, regional performance, product profitability, and client acquisition to diagnose root causes and enable data-driven decision-making.

Key Business Questions
How did sales and margins evolve year-over-year (2015–2023)?
Which regions and countries drive the most revenue and profit?
Which product categories and specific products are most / least profitable?
How is client acquisition trending, and which regions are losing customers?
What caused the sharp decline in 2023 compared to 2022?

Data Overview
Source: CSV – five files that provides data about: sales, product, customer, store, date

Analysis Scope
The Sales Report provides analysis across four dimensions: global, region, product, client
Time intelligence: 9-year trend analysis (2015–2023), YoY comparisons
Geographic breakdown: Continent (NA, EU, AU) and country-level (US, CA, DE, GB, FR, AU, NL, IT)
Product analytics: Category performance, Top 10 / Bottom 10 margin products,
Client analysis: Client count growth, new client acquisition, avg. order per client
Margin analysis: Margin %, margin by category, continent margin differences

Key Insights & Business Conclusions
Sales and margin trends:
2023 saw a sharp sales decline of −12.49M vs. 2022; margin fell by −7.23M despite a marginal +0.20% margin rate improvement
Peak sales occurred in 2022 (∼40M), suggesting a demand surge followed by correction
Regional performance:
North America dominates with $22M in sales — almost 2× Europe ($10M) and 7× Australia ($3M)
NA margin dropped by $5.5M in 2023 (−30.77%) — the primary driver of overall decline
Product insight:
Computers are the #1 revenue category ($12M); Music/Movies/AudioBooks lead margin % at 61.06%
Top margin products (66.89%) are Contoso DVD Storage Binders — high-value, low-cost items
Worst margins (∼48.96%) are accessories like phone screen protectors and hand games — consider repricing or delisting
Client analytics:
Client count peaked at 16.4K in 2022 and fell to 13.7K in 2023 — a loss of ∼2.7K clients
Average sales per client declining in AU and NA since 2021 — retention and upsell strategy needed
Recommendations:
Focus retention programs on North America and Australia where avg. order value is declining
Optimize or discontinue lowest-margin products; promote high-margin categories
Investigate 2023 demand drop in Computers segment — strongest category with biggest risk
